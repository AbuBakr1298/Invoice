Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about QuickInvoice features, privacy, and usage.

Is QuickInvoice really a free PDF invoice generator with no signup?

Yes. QuickInvoice is free, with no subscription, no account creation and no limit on how many A4 PDF invoices you create. Each invoice carries a small “Generated with QuickInvoice” line at the bottom of the page.

Where is my invoice data and customer information stored?

Invoice details are processed entirely in your browser. QuickInvoice does not store or track your financial data on any server. Nothing is saved between visits, so closing or reloading the tab clears the form: download your PDF before you leave.

Can I switch currency and add my company logo?

Yes. Choose USD, PKR, EUR, GBP, INR or AED, and add your logo by browsing for a file, dragging it in, pasting an image or entering an image URL. The currency symbol updates on every line and total, but QuickInvoice does not convert exchange rates, so enter amounts in the currency you are billing.

How does QuickInvoice calculate tax and discounts?

Each line total is quantity × rate, and the subtotal is the sum of the line totals. The discount percentage is taken off the subtotal first, then the tax percentage is applied to the discounted amount: total = (subtotal − discount) + tax. Every amount is rounded to the cent so the figures add up. Tax rules differ by country, so confirm the right rate for your situation. See our guide to calculating invoice tax and discounts for a worked example.

Is a no-signup invoice maker safe for client data?

Yes, in the way that matters most: the invoice is built and exported in your browser, so client names, addresses and bank details are not uploaded to our servers. Two small things to know: if you type an image URL for your logo or QR code, your browser fetches that image from the address you entered, and the PDF library is downloaded from a public CDN when you start filling in the form. Neither request includes your invoice data. As with any tool, avoid entering sensitive details on a shared or public computer.

Can I download, print or send the invoice?

Yes. Download an A4 PDF, print it from your browser (or choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog), share a prefilled message through WhatsApp, or copy a ready-made payment email and attach your PDF to it.

Does QuickInvoice work on mobile phones?

Yes. The layout is responsive, with an Edit Form tab and a Live PDF Preview tab on small screens. Fill in the form, switch to the preview and download the PDF from there.
