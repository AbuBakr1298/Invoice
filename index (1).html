About QuickInvoice

A free PDF invoice generator that is fast, simple and private.

QuickInvoice is a free, browser-based PDF invoice generator for freelancers, independent consultants, contractors and small business owners. Fill in your details, add line items, choose a currency and download a professional A4 invoice in minutes, with no account to create.

What you can do with it

  • Build an invoice with your logo, client details, line items, tax and discount, with a live A4 preview as you type.
  • Bill in US dollars, Pakistani rupees, euros, pounds, Indian rupees or UAE dirhams.
  • Download the invoice as a PDF, print it, share a prefilled message on WhatsApp, or copy a ready-made payment email.
  • Add bank details and an optional payment QR code so clients know how to pay.

How it handles your data

QuickInvoice runs in your browser. The details you type, including client names, addresses and bank details, are used to build the preview and the PDF on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers and nothing is saved between visits. The privacy policy explains the few cases where your browser contacts another service.

Free to use

There is no subscription, no account and no limit on how many invoices you create. Each invoice carries a small “Generated with QuickInvoice” line at the bottom of the page.

Learn the basics

We also publish practical invoicing guides: how to create a freelance invoice, calculate tax and discounts, choose payment terms, bill in other currencies and tell invoices, receipts and quotes apart.

Who is behind QuickInvoice

A note on advice

QuickInvoice is a tool, not an accountant or a lawyer. Invoice and tax requirements differ by country, so check the rules that apply to you before you rely on any invoice.
